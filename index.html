Prototype terms

This educational demo handles no real payments. Share only audio you own or have permission to use. A demo license is non-exclusive permission to use the listed file, not ownership of a person's voice. Rentals last 30 days and expiry blocks new downloads. Report listings that raise rights or consent concerns.

Prototype privacy information

Supabase stores account details, audio and demo activity. Full audio is private and available to its owner or valid license holders. Publishing shares recording metadata with signed-in users and makes the separate preview publicly accessible, including after a listing is archived. Your browser retains a sign-in session until logout. Use consented test audio and avoid sensitive personal content.
